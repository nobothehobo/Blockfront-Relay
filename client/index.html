iPhone / iPad
Left stick to move; push farther to sprint. Drag the right side to look, or drag SHOOT to aim while firing. Tap AIM to toggle zoom. Hold JUMP or JET; tap RELOAD or CROUCH. Tap the weapon name to choose directly. BUILD opens the digging/placement tools; SHOOT becomes PLACE or DIG for the selected tool. Tap BUILD again to return to your gun. With Blocks selected, tap KIT to cycle a cover wall, ramp, shelter, bridge or single block. Green ghosts are placeable; red ghosts show why placement is blocked.

Frontier forces
Choose a class before deploying, or queue a change using your class badge. Tap FRAG for a grenade and the named ability for your class skill. Desktop: G grenade, V ability, K field gear, 7 blast launcher (Sapper in specialist rooms). In Specialist loadouts each class has its own weapons; Skirmisher gets the jetpack when Class jetpacks is selected. Sappers dig twice as fast. Explosives can damage you as well as carve terrain.

Frontline Control
Stand within a sector ring for eight seconds to capture it. Teammates speed up capture; enemies contest it. Each held sector earns one point per second. First to 300 wins, or the higher score at the timer. Build approaches, cover and defenses around the sectors.

Supply stations
Return to your team's marked supply crate and stay nearby for three seconds to replenish health, ammunition, blocks and grenades. Recent damage interrupts resupply; each use has a 25-second cooldown. Zombies cannot use supplies.

Capture the flag
Steal the enemy banner and bring it home while your own flag is home. Touch your dropped flag to return it; it returns automatically after 25 seconds. First to three captures wins. NPC squads split into defenders and raiders, escort friendly carriers and recover stolen flags. Solo practice lets you select the map, mode, NPC count and round length; it still requires the game server. Outdoor maps have a six-minute day/night cycle shared by everyone. Lumen Quay Afterdark is a permanently moonlit neon city, available in both map menus.

Relay
Touch the enemy relay to collect it. Bring it to your base while your own relay is home. Dropped relays return after 25 seconds.

Outbreak
Humans survive five minutes. Eliminated humans become zombies. Zombies regenerate, jump higher, scramble up walls while holding jump, and dig faster. Reach the central beacon for pickup jetpacks.
