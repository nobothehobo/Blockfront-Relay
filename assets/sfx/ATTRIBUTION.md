# Sound effect credits and provenance

Source pages/licenses checked 2026-10-14. These clips may be redistributed in a game under their stated licenses. CC0 does not require attribution; credits are retained voluntarily. dklon’s CC BY 3.0 credit is required and shipped in the game credits and `client/public/sfx/ATTRIBUTION.txt`. No suggestion of creator endorsement.

All delivered sounds: mono, 32,000 Hz, signed 16-bit PCM WAV. Total audio bytes: 980544. Source downloads/extracted full libraries stay in scratch and are not shipped.

Footsteps: grass clips also represent dirt/sand; concrete clips represent stone/concrete. Death clips are brief human vocal grunts; `fall-*` are distinct soft impacts, not vocal recordings. Jetpack cues are edited jet/rocket engine effects from dklon’s takeoff effect, adapted for fictional jetpack thrust; source description includes Doppler shift, which remains audible in the sampled loop. They are not claimed to be recordings of a real wearable jetpack.

The firearm pack contains repeated shots: two separate shot excerpts from each selected near-distance recording create genuine variants, with tails retained.
