# Sound effect credits and provenance

Source pages and licenses checked 2026-10-15. CC0 permits game redistribution and commercial use; these voluntary credits are retained. dklon’s CC BY 3.0 credit is required. No suggestion of creator endorsement.

All delivered sounds are mono, 32,000 Hz, signed 16-bit PCM WAV. Original source archives stay outside the repository and are not shipped.

2.19 replaces all four weapon families with different live firearm recordings and fuller outdoor tails: AK-47 rifle, PPSh SMG, Benelli Nova pump shotgun, Savage .300 Blackout marksman. These are real recorded reports, adapted to fictional game weapons. No synthesized gunfire is baked into the new clips.

Reload phases use recorded mechanical foley: SpringySpringo recorded airsoft handling, zer0_sol supplied shell loading/racking, Brian MacIntosh supplied loading clicks. Airsoft recordings and adapted racking are not claimed to be live recordings of each fictional weapon model. Launcher and explosive cues use actual agricultural gas-cannon blasts by Joseph SARDIN, adapted to the game's fictional launcher/grenades; not recordings of grenade detonation. Sources are credited below even when optional.

Footsteps: grass represents dirt/sand; concrete represents stone/concrete. Death is human vocal grunts; fall clips are soft impacts. Jetpack uses dklon’s edited engine takeoff effect, not an actual wearable jetpack.
