iPhone / iPad
Left stick to move; push farther to sprint. Drag the right side to look, or drag SHOOT to aim while firing. Tap AIM to toggle zoom. Hold JUMP or JET; tap RELOAD or CROUCH. Tap the weapon name to choose directly. BUILD opens the digging/placement tools; SHOOT becomes PLACE or DIG for the selected tool. Tap BUILD again to return to your gun.

Frontier forces
Choose a class before deploying, or queue a change using your class badge. Tap FRAG for a grenade and the named ability for your class skill. Desktop: G grenade, V ability, 7 blast launcher. Explosives can damage you as well as carve terrain.

Relay
Touch the enemy relay to collect it. Bring it to your base while your own relay is home. Dropped relays return after 25 seconds.

Outbreak
Humans survive five minutes. Eliminated humans become zombies. Zombies regenerate, jump higher, scramble up walls while holding jump, and dig faster. Reach the central beacon for pickup jetpacks.
